Murat Atimtay is a computational artist and creative technologist exploring the relationships between people, technology, and the systems that connect them. Through generative images, plotter drawings, and interactive installations, he uses code to give ideas a visual and physical form. He is drawn to the rituals we form around machines and the ways we find meaning, presence, and connection in invisible digital processes.

His work has been shown in Times Square, London, Frankfurt, and Jerusalem, including an interactive installation at the Lights in Jerusalem Festival in 2017. In 2019, his algorithmic tattoo project, Heliantone, received first prize at the Frankfurt Tattoo Convention.
